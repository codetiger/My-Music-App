My Music App

A calm, distraction-free music player. Your songs stay on your phone and play without internet.

Download the App For Android 15 or newer

Install It

  1. Open this page in Chrome on the phone and tap Download the App. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.

  2. When it finishes, tap Open.

  3. If the phone says this source isn't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  4. Tap Install, then Open, and follow Phone Setup in the app.

On Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco phones, wait for the security check, then tap Install anyway. If it asks for a Mi account, tap Cancel and try again.
