# Avatar

The user's photo redrawn as a line drawing in a circle, or the music-note logo until they add one.

## Sizes
- 64dp (`size-avatar-lg`): beside the personalised title at the top of Home.
- 48dp (`size-avatar`): the Your Name and Photo row in Settings.
- 240dp (`size-art-hero`): the Use This / Try Again preview and Your Name and Photo.
- 432px adaptive bitmap for the home-screen shortcut, drawing inside the centre 66%.

## Rules
- Lines `drawing-ink` on `drawing-paper` with a thin ring of `drawing-ink`: the app's own `ink` and `surface`, so the drawing uses the same two colours as the app.
- Decorative at the top of Home and in Settings (TalkBack reads the title or row next to it). On its own screen: "Your photo drawing".
